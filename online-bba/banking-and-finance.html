Online BBA in Banking and Finance Program Overview

An Online BBA in Banking and Finance gives students a practical foundation in how the financial sector works. It introduces them to business principles, banking systems, and the flow of money across individuals and organizations. The aim is to help learners understand how financial decisions are made and why they matter.

Students can tailor their learning through elective tracks that focus on areas such as digital banking, investment services, and compliance. This flexibility allows them to build knowledge that aligns with their interests while still strengthening core business skills.

The specialization stays relevant because banks and financial institutions continue to evolve with new technologies, tighter regulations, and changing customer needs. The program prepares learners to navigate this environment with a clear understanding of how modern financial services operate.

  • UGC-entitled degree program
  • Earn a degree from NAAC A+ accredited university
  • Built-in Internship/Job opportunity with access to job portal with 300+ hiring partners

Know More About Online BBA in Banking and Finance

What You Will Learn With Banking and Finance: Core Electives

Below is a simple overview of the electives offered in this specialization and the practical value each subject adds to a learner's understanding of the financial world -

Retail and Digital Banking: Students learn how banks support everyday customers through savings accounts, credit products, and digital platforms. They study how service models are built and how customer checks are handled. This subject helps them understand the systems behind familiar banking experiences.

Commercial Banking Operations: This elective explains how banks evaluate business clients, assess credit needs, and manage lending for companies. Students review financial statements and learn how banks judge risk. It gives clarity on how commercial banks support the backbone of the economy.

Investment Banking and Financial Services: Learners explore mergers, acquisitions, equity offerings, and advisory work. They follow how investment banks structure major deals and manage corporate transactions. This subject shows them how high-stakes financial decisions are planned and executed.

Wealth and Portfolio Management: Students learn how investment portfolios are designed, balanced, and reviewed. They study risk tolerance, asset choices, and long-term planning for different types of investors. It strengthens their ability to advise on financial goals with more structure and confidence.

Financial Technology (FinTech) and Banking Innovation: This subject introduces the tools that modernize banking, from digital payments to automated lending. Students explore how data and technology improve efficiency and access. It prepares them to understand the future direction of financial services.

Blockchain and Cryptocurrency in Banking: Students study decentralized systems, secure transactions, and digital assets. They explore blockchain use cases in banks and gain a grounded view of cryptocurrency markets. This elective helps them understand one of the most disruptive forces in finance.

Financial Management for Startups and SMEs: This subject focuses on how small businesses plan budgets, raise funds, and manage cash flow. Students learn practical methods used by entrepreneurs to stay financially stable. It prepares them to support new ventures with structured financial thinking.

Banking Laws & Compliance (RBI, SEBI, FEMA, IFRS): Learners study the rules that guide financial institutions and protect market integrity. They understand reporting standards, foreign exchange policies, and conduct regulations. This subject develops the discipline needed for compliance-based roles.
