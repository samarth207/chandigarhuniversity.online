Program Overview

The Online Bachelor of Business Administration in Business Analytics is a three-year undergraduate program that provides learners with the necessary business skills for today's and tomorrow's markets. Through extensive use of analytical tools, this program intends to train learners to analyse complex business problems and assist in critical decision-making. Chandigarh University Online BBA in Business Analytics is a one-of-a-kind course that helps learners interested in exploring the conceptual side of business and gaining expertise in data-driven business solutions.

This online BBA in Business Analytics program, offered in collaboration with KPMG, provides learners with hands-on experience in data analytics and the business industry, positioning them for enhanced career opportunities such as Business Analyst, Data Analyst, Machine Learning Engineer, Statistician, and various other roles.
