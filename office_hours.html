What Are Office Hours?

Office Hours are designated times when our faculty members are available to provide one-on-one or small group academic support. These sessions are designed to:

  • Address course-specific queries.
  • Offer guidance on assignments, projects, or case studies.
  • Provide insights into real-world applications of academic concepts.
  • Help you strategize for exams or assessments.

Key Features of Our Office Hours

Convenient Scheduling

Flexible slots to suit your busy schedule, ensuring you never miss the opportunity to get the support you need.

Personalized Attention

Small group or individual interactions allow for tailored guidance to meet your unique learning requirements.

Expert Faculty

Engage with experienced educators and industry professionals who bring practical insights and academic expertise.

Accessible Online Format

Connect via user-friendly online platforms, ensuring seamless access from anywhere in the world.

All Programs Covered

Office Hours are available for all our online degree programs, ensuring comprehensive support for every learner.

How to Join Office Hours

  1. Check the Schedule

    Log in to your Student Portal to view the weekly Office Hours schedule for your courses.

  2. Book a Slot

    Reserve your spot for the session you wish to attend. Early booking is recommended.

  3. Prepare Your Queries

    Make the most of your session by listing your questions beforehand.

  4. Join the Session

    Use the provided meeting link to join your scheduled session.

Benefits of Participating

  • Improved academic performance through targeted support.
  • Enhanced understanding of complex topics.
  • Opportunities to discuss ideas and gain feedback from faculty.
  • Building a rapport with professors and peers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Are Office Hours mandatory?
No, participation in Office Hours is optional but highly encouraged for students seeking additional academic support.
