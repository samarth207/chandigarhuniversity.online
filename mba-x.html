MBA X - In Collaboration With Harvard Business School Online

Program Overview

Chandigarh University Online (CU Online) is proud to announce its collaboration with Harvard Business School Online (HBS Online) to integrate the prestigious Harvard Credential of Readiness (CORe) program into our MBA X Online Degree Program. This transformative initiative is designed to provide students with unparalleled access to world-class business education, fostering the skills needed to excel in today's competitive global market.

The two-year MBA X program is tailored for modern professionals aiming to secure leadership positions in the corporate world or drive innovation as entrepreneurs. Combining the rigorous curriculum designed by the esteemed faculty of Chandigarh University Online with the cutting-edge resources and pedagogy of Harvard Business School Online, this program offers dual specialization and an exceptional learning experience.

  • Delivered through an interactive and engaging online platform.
  • Features case studies, real-world applications, and insights from Harvard Business School faculty.
  • Earn a prestigious Credential of Readiness from HBS Online upon successful completion.
  • Supported by CU Online's robust digital learning framework and dedicated academic mentorship.

With a focus on hands-on learning, the HBS Online CORe program equips learners to excel in decision-making, advanced data analysis, financial management, and leadership in dynamic business environments. By the end of this program, participants will have undergone a transformative educational experience, built on a foundation of leadership, innovation, and astute business acumen provided by two globally renowned institutions.

This specialized program is more than just an MBA; it is a gateway to a future of endless possibilities in the global business landscape.

Minimum Eligibility

Applicants must have a minimum of 2 years of work experience in an organization or a CGPA of 6.0 or above in their undergraduate studies, regardless of the field of study.

What is the HBS Online CORe Program?

The Credential of Readiness (CORe) is a comprehensive program developed by Harvard Business School Online to equip professionals and aspiring leaders with a solid foundation in business essentials. This program focuses on three core business disciplines:

Business Analytics

Introduces quantitative methods used to analyze data and make better management decisions. Students will hone an understanding of key concepts, managerial judgement, and be confident applying course concepts to real business problems.

Economics for Managers

Dives into fundamental economic principles like supply and demand, cost, markets, competition, and differentiation. Students will gain the knowledge and skills needed to craft successful business strategy.

Financial Accounting

Will teach the fundamentals of financial accounting. Starting with the basics of recording transactions as journal entries and posting time to T accounts, students will learn how to prepare a balance sheet, income statement, and cash flow statement.

    HBS Online CORe Final Exam

    The CORe final exam is a three-hour, multiple-choice, closed book assessment created by HBS faculty and delivered via the HBS Online course platform. The exam is offered to participants after successful completion of the program during a five-day exam window two weeks after the last module deadline. Participants do not need to make appointments to take the final exam.

    The exam is divided into three parts � Business Analytics, Economics for Managers, and Financial Accounting � and you have 60 minutes to complete each section. Each of the three exam sections will feature 45 questions, most of which will be multiple-choice or multiple-selection. You may complete the exams in any order, and do not need to complete all sections in one sitting. Once you begin a section, however, a timer will start, and you'll be graded based on the number of questions completed in that period.

    Note: You'll be required to complete each question on the exam before you can proceed to the following one, and you may not edit your answers once submitted. You will not be able to "skip" questions and come back to them. The experience will be similar to your end-of-module quizzes, except that you will not be permitted to use your personal notes or any other study aid, nor will you be permitted to access the CORe course content while taking the exam. You may only take the exam once.
