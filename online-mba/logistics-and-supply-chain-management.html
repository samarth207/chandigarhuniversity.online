MBA in Logistics and Supply Chain Management Overview

Global supply chains are under constant pressure, with volatile fuel prices, manufacturing delays, fragmented supplier networks, and customers expecting faster deliveries. For businesses, the challenge is no longer "how to move goods" but how to build systems that absorb uncertainty without slowing growth.

Chandigarh University Online addresses this need with a future-focused MBA that lets learners tailor their path through multiple specializations. For those working in operations, warehousing, logistics, or vendor coordination without formal training, this flexibility becomes especially valuable.

The online MBA in Logistics and Supply Chain Management (LSCM) specialization is designed for learners who want to understand how products move across global markets. This also helps in building strong managerial and analytical capabilities. It bridges core business management with operational, logistical, and procurement-based decision making.

  • UGC-entitled degree program
  • Earn a degree from NAAC A+ accredited university
  • Built-in Internship/Job opportunity with access to job portal with 300+ hiring partners
  • Cost-effective with EMI options

Minimum Eligibility

Bachelor degree in any discipline from recognized University/ Institution. Graduates of recognized professional programs like CA/ ICWA etc., are also eligible to apply.

Know More About Online MBA in Logistics and Supply Chain Management

What You Will Learn: Core Electives

This specialization builds a strong foundation in operational strategy, data-backed planning, and end-to-end product movement across supply chain networks. Learners get to explore:

Logistics management: Learners study how goods move across networks and how logistics systems are structured. The subject introduces transport planning, fleet coordination, routing decisions, distribution models, and the fundamentals of building reliable logistics operations.

Quality management and quality techniques: Students learn how quality tools are applied, how deviations are identified, and how frameworks like Lean and Six Sigma support continuous improvement and operational consistency.

Logistics and supply chain management: You'll get a broader understanding of end-to-end supply chains. Learners explore supplier relationships, inventory planning, warehouse flows, sourcing decisions, and the coordination required to keep supply chains efficient and responsive.

Quantitative techniques for management: Students work with analytical tools used in decision-making. The subject covers forecasting models, demand estimation, probability concepts, statistical analysis, and performance metrics that support operational planning.

Retail store and visual merchandising: You'll learn how retail environments influence buying behavior. This section covers product placement, store layout, visual cues, inventory movement, and how retail operations impact overall supply chain performance.

Operations management: Students get to explore how operations are designed and managed inside an organisation. They learn about production processes, capacity utilization, procurement planning, workflow mapping, and cross-functional alignment for smooth operations.

Why Choose an Online MBA in Logistics and Supply Chain Management (LSCM)?

Modern supply chains rely on agility, data-backed planning, and integrated workflows across sourcing, warehousing, transport, and distribution. With AI-enabled forecasting, IoT-driven tracking, and automated workflows redefining sourcing, warehousing, transport, and distribution, today's logistics professionals must be fluent in both operational and technological systems.

The online MBA in Logistics and Supply Chain Management (LSCM) helps learners develop leadership abilities grounded in business fundamentals while building strong competence in technology-driven supply chain systems. It also offers domain-specific knowledge of logistics networks, vendor management, demand forecasting, inventory systems, and quality frameworks.

This makes graduates well-equipped to contribute to high-impact roles across retail, manufacturing, e-commerce, consulting, and international trade.

The program blends core management theory with real-world applications, allowing professionals to understand both the strategic and operational layers of supply chain design.
